About Us

Lucknow's Most Trusted Multi-Specialist Dental Clinic

Dr. Harpreet Singh established Happi Smiles Dental Clinic with a singular vision: to bring U.S.-standard dental care to Lucknow. What sets this clinic apart is having a complete team of MDS specialists - all working collaboratively under one roof.

Our multidisciplinary team includes:

  • Endodontist - Root Canal Treatments & Fillings
  • Orthodontist - Braces & Aligners
  • Oral Surgeon - Extractions, Implants & Fracture Cases
  • Pedodontist - Children's Dental Care
  • Prosthodontist - Dentures & Restorations
  • Periodontist - Gum Disease & Deep Cleaning
  • Oral Medicine Specialist - Pre-Cancerous Lesions & Rare Conditions

This integrated approach means unparalleled accuracy in diagnosis, minimized human error, and truly world-class care - all in Indira Nagar.
